Hold the Wall!

The orcs are coming. They are not clever, but there are a great many of them, and they march in neat blocks like bricks.

  • Move the mouse to slide the great royal shield along the wall. Every boulder the orc catapults throw that you block bounces back as one of your balls. The gold Royal Ball is yours from the start: click to serve it.
  • Where a ball meets the shield sets its angle. The centre sends it straight, the sides send it wide, and the very tips make trick shots.
  • Left click or Space right as a ball lands to heave: a power shot that smashes through a whole orc line.
  • Chain hits without touching the shield for a combo gold multiplier. Hits behind the lines pay double, the towers make bank shots and barrel orcs blow up their friends. Catch falling capsules, but not the red ones.
  • Every ball or boulder you miss hits the wall. If any one stretch of wall falls, the castle is breached.
  • At the War Council place defenders on the castle’s fixed spots. Each one covers only part of the field, and the red hatching shows your blind spots. Plan well: a good plan matters more than quick hands.
