Castle Breach

The orcs are coming. They are not clever, but there are a great many of them.

  • Your crossbow on the wall fires by itself at the closest orc.
  • Move the mouse to slide your shield along the wall. Hold Left click or Space to raise it and bounce chucker rocks back into the horde. It drains while raised.
  • Raise the shield right as a rock arrives for a perfect parry: it flies back three times as hard.
  • Right click or F brings down the hand of the castle on whatever is under the cursor. Each slap costs one Vigor.
  • Orcs that reach the wall climb it and hack at it. If any one stretch of wall falls, the castle is breached.
  • Kills earn gold. Spend it at the War Council, before the first wave and after every third: archers, catapults, a soldier line, upgrades and wall repairs.
  • Call waves early (Enter) for bonus gold and Renown. Hold for 25 waves; warlords lead waves 10, 20 and 25.
